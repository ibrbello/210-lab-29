My program will simulate a corporation's workforce over time (BelloCorp). The keys of the map will represent various 
departments at the company, such as the sales department, finance, product development, marketing, etc.
The array within the value of the map will contain the names of all the employees in that department.
The employees are of three types: Manager, Entry-Level, and Intern, corresponding to the three lists within
the array.

Events:
There are two main events: a hiring surge and a layoff. 
Hiring Surge: A random number of employees are added to a random department.
Layoff: A random number of employees are removed from a random department.
More events may be added in future updates.

At any given time period, either a lay off, hiring surge, or no event may occur. 
Each time period represents a quarter of the fiscal year. This simulation will simulate an 10-year period,
between January 2026 and December 2035, so there will be 40 time periods.
